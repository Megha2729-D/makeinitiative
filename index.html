About M.A.K.E. Initiative

Our Story

M.A.K.E. Initiative Foundation is a nonprofit organization founded on the belief that the strongest work is work that leaves something behind: greater access, greater knowledge, greater capacity, and stronger partnerships.

M.A.K.E. Initiative Foundation is built around action and execution. Healthcare is an important part of our Foundation, but meaningful change goes beyond immediate intervention. Education and training can build knowledge and capacity. Partnerships can extend what is possible. Resources, infrastructure and community support can help strengthen the environments in which people live and work.

Education and training can build knowledge and capacity. Partnerships can extend what is possible. Resources, infrastructure and community support can help strengthen the environments in which people live and work.

Our work may take different forms, but the purpose remains the same: to take on work that matters and make it impactful.

Our first initiative is only the beginning.

We don’t do this work alone. Donors, volunteers, professionals, businesses and partners each contribute something that can help make an initiative possible.

Where We Focus

Access to quality healthcare is not equal everywhere. In many communities, the challenge is not simply having a healthcare facility, it’s having the expertise, equipment, supplies and support needed to provide the right care. M.A.K.E. Initiative Foundation works with healthcare professionals, institutions and partners to help bridge these gaps and make meaningful care possible.

Dr. Kim Le MD, MPH, FRCSC
Dr. Kim le is a highly skilled refractive cataract surgeon and comprehensive ophthalmologist with extensive clinical and surgical experience. Throughout her career, she has held leadership roles while remaining committed to providing thoughtful, personalized patient care. Her Expertise in cataract surgery and commitment to expanding access to quality eye care are an important part of M.A.K.E. Initiative Foundation’s healthcare work. As Medical Director, Dr Le provides clinical leadership and perspective to initiatives focused on expanding access to specialized healthcare, advancing surgical education and supporting, sustainable impact in the communities we serve.
Marcus Saroop

Director of Business Development & Media

Marcus brings extensive experience in humanitarian initiatives, international development, fundraising, and coordinating complex aid efforts in challenging environments. His work has involved developing programs to support underserved and vulnerable communities, identifying community needs, building relationships with organizations and agencies, and coordinating the resources required to respond effectively. His experience spans from a range of humanitarian and infrastructure initiatives, including healthcare, construction, emergency response, water and sanitation, transportation, and community development. He has also developed strong relationships with executive, governmental, military, and international aid organizations. At M.A.K.E. Initiative, Marcus serves as Director of Business Development & Media, helping communicate the organization’s work and document the people, partnerships, and initiatives behind it. He brings a perspective shaped by experience in the field and a belief that meaningful work should not only be carried out, but understood, shared, and supported.
Genelle George

Founder & Executive Director

Genelle George is the Founder& Executive Director of M.A.K.E. Initiative Foundation,bringing more than a decade of experience in healthcare, clinical operations, and community service. Before founding M.A.K.E. Initiative Foundation, Genelle spent 10 years working in ophthalmology, as a Clinical Director. Her experience working alongside physicians and surgical teams gave her a firsthand understanding of the resources, coordination and collaboration required to deliver specialized healthcare. Her work with M.A.K.E. Initiative Foundation grew from a desire to take that experience beyond the clinical setting and apply it to initiatives where practical support, strong partnerships and thoughtful execution can make a meaningful difference. As Founder & Executive Director, Genelle leads the organization’s operations, partnerships and initiatives, working to turn needs into organized, actionable projects and ensuring that the resources and people involved are brought together to see the work through.

Frequently Asked Questions

M.A.K.E. Initiative is a nonprofit organization focused on mobilizing access, knowledge and expertise to help address important needs in underserved and vulnerable communities.

Healthcare is at the heart of M.A.K.E. Initiative. Our work extends beyond direct care to include education and training, partnerships, community support, resources and infrastructure, and responses to emerging needs.

The Grenada Cataract Mission is M.A.K.E. Initiative's inaugural initiative. It brings together a specialized clinical team, healthcare partners and the support needed to expand access to cataract surgery and help prevent avoidable blindness in Grenada. The mission is planned for October 2026.

There are many ways to support M.A.K.E. Initiative, including volunteering, providing professional expertise, donating supplies or equipment, becoming a corporate supporter, supporting an initiative, or joining a mission.

Donors, volunteers, healthcare professionals, businesses, organizations and community partners can each contribute resources, expertise or support to help make an initiative possible.
