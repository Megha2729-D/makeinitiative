Restoring Sight. Expanding Possibility.

For many patients in Grenada, access to cataract surgery has been delayed for years, with a backlog that grew during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Limited availability of surgical resources, equipment and appropriate lenses can mean waiting even longer for the care that could restore their sight and independence.
