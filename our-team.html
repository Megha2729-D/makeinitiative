Our Team

Meet the dedicated professionals leading M.A.K.E. Initiative and turning resources into meaningful action.

Dr. Kim Le MD, MPH, FRCSC

Medical Director

Dr. Kim le is a highly skilled refractive cataract surgeon and comprehensive ophthalmologist with extensive clinical and surgical experience. Throughout her career, she has held leadership roles and remained committed to providing thoughtful, personalized patient care.

Her Expertise in cataract surgery and her commitment to expanding access to quality eye care are an important part of M.A.K.E. Initiative’s healthcare work. As Medical Director, Dr Le brings clinical leadership and perspective to initiatives focused on improving access to specialized healthcare work. As Medical Director, Dr. Le brings clinical leadership and perspective to initiatives focused on improving access to specialized healthcare and supporting meaningful, sustainable impact in the communities we serve.

Marcus Saroop

Director of Business Development & Media

Marcus brings extensive experience in humanitarian initiatives, international development, fundraising, and coordinating complex aid efforts in challenging environments. His work has involved developing programs to support underserved and vulnerable communities, identifying community needs, building relationships with organizations and agencies, and coordinating the resources required to respond effectively.

His experience spans from a range of humanitarian and infrastructure initiatives, including healthcare, construction, emergency response, water and sanitation, transportation, and community development. He has also developed strong relationships with executive, governmental, military, and international aid organizations.

At M.A.K.E. Initiative, Marcus serves as Media Director, helping communicate the organization’s work and document the people, partnerships, and initiatives behind it. He brings a perspective shaped by experience in the field and a belief that meaningful work should not only be carried out, but understood, shared, and supported.

Genelle George

Founder & Executive Director

Genelle George is the Founder of M.A.K.E. Initiative. Bringing together more than a decade of experience in healthcare, clinical operations, and community service. /p>

Before founding M.A.K.E., Genelle spent 10 years working in ophthalmology, as a Clinical Director. Her experience working alongside physicians and surgical teams gave her a firsthand understanding of the resources, coordination and collaboration required to deliver specialized healthcare.

Her work with M.A.K.E. grew from a desire to take that experience beyond the clinical setting and apply it to initiatives where practical support, strong partnerships and thoughtful execution can make a meaningful difference.

As Founder & Executive Director, Genelle leads the organization’s operations, partnerships and initiatives, working to turn needs into organized, actionable projects and ensuring that the resources and people involved are brought together to see the work through.
